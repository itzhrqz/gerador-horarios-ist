The Gerador de Horários | IST appeared so that planning the next semester's schedule is a piece of cake!

It lets you find the best schedule based on your needs and courses, and save it in a PDF format so you can check it out anytime. You can also coordinate shifts with your colleagues by pinning classes.

Due to IST change to semester-based schedules and the original author's unavailability, I decided to take over this project and give it an emergency update, this is by no means a finished or polished version but it already fixes the out of memory errors and also allows to choose classes from multiple shifts (stole it from a guy who made a pull request some years ago lol).
